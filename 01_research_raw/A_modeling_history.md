## How modeling works (plain)
Tube amp chain: preamp triodes (most distortion) → tone stack (passive filter) → phase inverter → power tubes → output transformer → speaker cab → mic. Each stage partly nonlinear and interacting (Hotz, TU Graz 2011: https://www2.spsc.tugraz.at/www-archive/downloads/tube_amp_modeling.pdf).
1. Behavioural/block (grey-box): filters + waveshapers + cab IR. Neural DSP researchers: "an ensemble of LTI digital filters and static nonlinear functions" needing "careful circuit analysis and empirical adjustment" (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.08559). Line 6 1997 patent is early example (8x oversampling).
2. Component-level / circuit simulation (white-box): nodal / state-space / DK-method (Yeh, Stanford 2009–2012); Wave Digital Filters (Karjalainen & Pakarinen ICASSP 2006). "computationally prohibitive for complex circuits".
3. Commercial practice = hybrid. Cliff Chase (2011): no "static transfer functions"; triode models have "memory"; "It's not magic" (https://guitarmessenger.com/cliff-chase-interview-fractal-audio/). Line 6's Ben Adrian (2025): presence knob "controlling how the feedback happens in the power amp just like in the real amp"; with a profile, changing parameters "the tone or gain isn't going to change like it would on an amp in the real world" (https://blog.line6.com/2025/05/28/ben-adrian-on-the-art-and-craft-of-component-modeling/).
"Feel" ingredients: sag, negative feedback/presence/resonance, speaker impedance interaction, bias excursion (Fractal Cygnus), mains ripple (Line 6 measured 50 vs 60 Hz, SoS 2006).

## Who was first vs who popularised
- First analog amp simulation: Redmere Soloist 1979 (weak), Rockman 1982; SansAmp 1989 most often credited.
- First digital tube emulation on paper: Pritchard patent filed 1988.
- First commercial digital amp modeling: conflict. Roland VG-8 (1995) digitally modeled amp/speaker/mic under COSM (but guitar-modeling system w/ special pickup). Line 6 AxSys 212 (1996) "first modeling guitar amp" (Ryle 2013: https://www.kvraudio.com/interviews/innovation-driver-interview-with-marcus-ryle-22266). Roland GP-100 digital processor predated both (cited in Line 6 patent). Fair framing: Roland first to digitally model an amp inside a product; Line 6 first to build a guitar amp around modeling.
- Popularised: Line 6 POD 1998 (+ Amp Farm in studios).
- Pro-grade: Fractal Axe-Fx 2006.
- Mass market: Boss Katana 2016, Positive Grid Spark 2019/20, Fender Tone Master. Reverb 2025 data (via Wikipedia): top-5 best-selling amp models all digital; traditional amps still outsold modelers 4:1 overall.
